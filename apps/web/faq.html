Questions

Frequently asked.

Short answers, no marketing. If something here is unclear, that's a bug — tell us.

Does FeatherKey send my typing anywhere?
No. The app requests no internet permission, so it is technically incapable of transmitting anything. There is no server, no account and no analytics.
What does "learning" actually store?
Words and short word pairs you type often, plus tap-accuracy statistics — held in an encrypted store on your device. Not messages, not full sentences, not anything from password or payment fields.
Is learning on by default?
Yes, but you can turn it off any time. You're asked once, on first launch and can change it on the settings. The keyboard is fully usable if you never turn it on.
How do I delete what it learned?
Settings → "Clear learned data". It removes the store immediately and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
Why do two languages say "layout only"?
Русский and Ελληνικά have keyboard layouts but no bundled word list yet, so you get the keys without suggestions or autocorrect. We label it rather than quietly degrade.
Can I use more than one language at a time?
Yes — tick as many as you like and suggestions blend across them. At least one must stay selected.
Does voice input stay on-device?
Voice hands off to whichever recogniser your phone provides, and that service's own privacy terms apply. FeatherKey never sees or stores the audio.
Why does Android warn me when I enable a keyboard?
Android shows that warning for every input method, because keyboards can see what you type. It is not specific to FeatherKey — and it is exactly why we ship without network access.
What does it cost?
Nothing. There is no paid tier, no subscription and no advertising.
